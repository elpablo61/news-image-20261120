## The five stories

1. **Summit of Three** — Donald Trump, Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin hold a historic
   three-way meeting in China, talking nuclear risks, energy security and the wars.
2. **ICJ: Myanmar Guilty of Genocide** — the International Court of Justice rules that
   Myanmar's military intended to destroy a substantial part of the Rohingya minority.
3. **Cyclone Akvan Slams India** — a severe cyclone barrels into the Odisha coast;
   hundreds of thousands evacuated as heavy rain and huge waves hit.
4. **Voyager 1: One Light-Day Out** — NASA confirms the Voyager 1 probe has reached a
   full light-day from Earth, still phoning home from interstellar space.
5. **GTA 6 Smashes Records** — Grand Theft Auto VI, released 19 November, already
   passes The Odyssey's $1.80 billion global run.

## How it works

`entry.png` (1024×1024, JPEG data) is the image, generated fresh for this day's
news — it is the agent's own work, not a reused picture. `main.ts` copies it to
`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`) when run with Node 22, and verifies the
copy landed, reporting its size:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
